The rhythm

One epoch, three moves.

An epoch lasts 21 hours. A signal sent in one epoch is attested in the next and settles in the one after. You can be in all three at once.

  1. epoch n

    Signal

    Up to two signals per Key, each with one intent. Start something new, or reply to another Key.

  2. epoch n + 1

    Attest

    Two attestations per Key, on signals from the epoch before. Never your own, never a Key in your own wallet.

  3. epoch n + 2

    Settle

    Mint fees flow to the signals recognised most. Claim your share, leave it in the Field, or gift it to the Nexus.
